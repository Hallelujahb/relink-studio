# Accessibility

We want Relink Studio to be usable by as many people as possible, but we haven't done a formal accessibility audit yet, so we won't claim conformance with any standard.

## Supported environments

Relink Studio runs locally and is used through a modern desktop browser (current Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari).

## Known limitations

- The review queue is dense, and keyboard and screen reader support hasn't been systematically tested.
- Color is used to mark review kinds, and may not be enough on its own for everyone.
- Non-Latin scripts (Amharic, Arabic, Chinese) are matched correctly, but the interface itself is English only.

## Reporting a barrier

If something is hard or impossible to use, please open an issue titled "Accessibility: ..." and tell us what you were trying to do, what got in the way, and which browser and assistive tech you used. We'll treat it as a real bug.
