# Code of Conduct

Relink Studio is a small project, and we'd like to keep it a pleasant place to work on. Be kind, assume good faith, and keep the conversation about the work.

## What we expect

- Be respectful, even when you disagree.
- Give feedback on the code or the idea, not the person.
- Be patient with newcomers. Everyone started somewhere.
- Own your mistakes and say so when you've made one.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing someone's private information
- Deliberately derailing discussions

## Where this applies

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where you are representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

If something's wrong, email hallelujahEzra@gmail.com. Reports are handled privately and taken seriously. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or ban people who break these rules.

This is a short, plain-language take on the ideas in the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
